Root Cause Analysis

Kya Ho Raha Hai Actually?

Ye ek single problem nahi — ye chain reaction hai jo cascading effects me baaki sab trigger karti gayi

🏥 Heavy Antibiotics (Balaji) — 10 goli/day, injections
↓
💀 ALL Gut Bacteria DESTROYED (Good + Bad)
↓
⚠️ GUT DYSBIOSIS — Intestinal Ecosystem Tabah
↓

4 Cascading Effects

1. Malabsorption
  • Nutrients absorb nahi ho rahe
  • Calcium ↓ → Bone pain, cracking
  • Vit D ↓ → Cold intolerance
  • Iron ↓ → Weakness, dizziness
  • Magnesium ↓ → Anxiety, cramps
2. Leaky Gut
  • Intestinal lining damaged
  • Systemic inflammation
  • Joint pain, bone pain
  • Period problems worsen
  • Estrobolome disrupted
3. Gut-Brain Axis Broken
  • 90% serotonin gut me banta hai
  • Hypothalamus signals weak
  • Anxiety, insomnia, depression
  • Mood swings, numbness
  • Motility ↓ (30-40%)
4. Immune Collapse
  • 70% immune system gut me hai
  • Infections easily lag jaati
  • Temperature regulation gone
  • "Andar se hollow" feel
  • Healing slow
Why B12 Normal But Still Weak?

B12 aa raha hai, but doosre nutrients absorb nahi ho rahe: Iron (heavy periods + malabsorption), Vitamin D, Calcium, Magnesium, Folate, Zinc — sab likely deficient hain.

Why Bael Se 70% Thik Hui Thi?

Bael (Aegle marmelos) directly root cause pe kaam karta hai: anti-inflammatory, anti-diarrheal, prebiotic (good bacteria grow), intestinal lining coat/protect, bad bacteria selectively kill. Isliye bael season me 70% thik thi!

Apollo ne IBS bola — lekin...

IBS ek "diagnosis of exclusion" hai — jab kuch aur na mile toh bol dete hain. Sahityaka ke symptoms IBS se kaafi zyada severe hain. Important conditions miss ho gayi hain — SIBO, Celiac, Hypothyroidism test nahi hue.
